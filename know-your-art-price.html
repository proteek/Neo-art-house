Pricing methodology

What the recommendation considers.

Artist market position

Career stage, exhibitions, representation, institutional exposure and documented demand.

Existing sales evidence

Previous direct sales, gallery sales, commissions and pricing consistency.

Artwork characteristics

Medium, dimensions, object type, edition, fabrication, series and production complexity.

Career momentum

Recent exhibitions, awards, residencies, publications and meaningful market activity.

Comparable context

Relevant artists and works used as context rather than as a mechanical price copy.

Geography & channel

Direct sale, gallery retail, commission work and international positioning can require different pricing logic.

What we do not do: we do not authenticate artwork, promise future resale value, or treat social-media popularity as market value.
