What this record means

Artist-issued means an issuer registered these details and a PDF fingerprint. It does not verify the issuer’s identity, authorship, ownership, authenticity, provenance, price or payment. A matching file is the exact registered digital file; a matching QR alone is insufficient.

A copied QR can appear on a different document. Compare your complete PDF above. For paper copies, check the record details and request the original digital PDF.
