Independence

We work for the buyer.
Nobody else pays us.

Our business model is structured to keep the research independent of the transaction counterparties.

The principle is simple.

No auction commission. No dealer commission. No referral fees. No financial interest in what you buy.

Auction housesWe do not accept transaction commissions or preferential-placement payments.
Galleries and dealersWe do not take seller-side commissions or pay-to-promote placement.
Shippers, insurers and other service providersWe do not accept referral fees for sending you to them.
Lot reviewsCharged as fixed-fee written research, independent of hammer price.
