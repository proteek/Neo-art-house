AESTUM / LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Clear terms for your artwork, your practice and your choices.

1. Who we are and scope

AESTUM is an artist-practice product of The Neo Art House. This policy covers the AESTUM iPhone app, its product page and enquiries about AESTUM. Contact us at connect@theneoarthouse.com for privacy questions or requests. Other Neo Art House website services may have different data flows and are not described as part of the app here.

2. Artwork and studio information

The current app processes selected artwork images using on-device image analysis. Artist profiles, artwork details, saved images, working prices, career information and document drafts are stored locally where you save them in the app. No AESTUM account is required. We do not receive this local content merely because you use the app. Your device and backup settings may cause Apple to include app data in a device backup.

3. Photos, camera and exports

You choose which image to provide. Camera access is requested when needed to photograph artwork; you can decline or change permissions in iOS Settings. Exporting or sharing a document sends it only to the destination you select. Copies in Files, Photos, email or other apps are controlled separately from AESTUM. Avoid including personal information about others unless you have permission.

4. Internet requests and reference images

When you load museum references, the app requests public metadata and images from the Art Institute of Chicago and The Metropolitan Museum of Art. Their servers receive the network information necessary to respond, such as your IP address and requested resource. The current museum-reference requests do not upload your artwork image or studio profile; visual comparisons run on the device. Opening a source link takes you to that provider’s website and privacy practices.

5. Cloud features

Cloud artwork-image search and remote market-evidence queries are not enabled in the current release configuration. We will update this policy with the provider, information sent, purpose and retention before enabling those services, and request permission before sending an artwork image to a cloud service. The presence of a planned feature is not consent to upload your work.

6. Purchases and Apple services

Apple processes in-app purchases. AESTUM reads StoreKit product and verified transaction information, including subscription status and expiration, to provide or restore access. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple handles billing and maintains its transaction records under its own privacy policy. TestFlight testing may also provide Apple’s crash, diagnostic and tester-feedback information to the developer under the TestFlight terms; do not include sensitive information in feedback.

7. Website visits and support

The product and legal pages are hosted through Cloudflare, which processes network and request information to deliver and secure the website. These AESTUM pages do not add advertising trackers or analytics scripts. If you email us, we receive your address, message and attachments and use them to answer your enquiry, administer requested early-access communications or address a privacy request. Do not email payment-card information. You can ask us to stop early-access communications at any time.

8. Sharing and protection

We do not sell your artwork or personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or use your private artwork to train a shared AI model. We use service providers only as necessary to deliver hosting, communications and support, with protections appropriate to the data and consistent with this policy and applicable requirements. Information may also be disclosed where legally required or necessary to protect rights and security. Apple and independently visited museum services operate under their own policies. Providers may process information outside your country, subject to applicable safeguards. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

9. Retention and deletion

Local studio records remain until removed from the app or the app is deleted. To remove the app’s local data, choose Delete App in iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage → AESTUM; Offload App retains documents and data. Delete exported copies and device backups separately through their respective services. Museum caches may remain until the system clears them or you delete the app. We retain support emails only while needed to resolve the request or meet legal obligations, then delete them. Hosting security records follow the hosting provider’s applicable retention settings. Apple retains purchase records under its own policy. Deleting AESTUM does not cancel a subscription.

10. Your choices and rights

You can decline photo or camera access, avoid optional reference downloads, stop using a feature, or request deletion of information you sent us by emailing connect@theneoarthouse.com. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, obtain a copy, withdraw consent, and complain to your local data-protection authority. We may request proportionate verification before acting. Withdrawal does not change the lawfulness of earlier processing. We cannot remotely retrieve or erase information held only on your device.

11. Children and changes

AESTUM is intended for artists and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address and delete it where required. We will publish updates here with a revised date and provide additional notice or seek consent when required for material changes.
