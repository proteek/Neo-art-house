The role

We investigate the transaction around the artwork.

Cross-border collecting creates a practical problem: auction-house charges, bank rules, customs, export restrictions, cultural-property law, insurance and shipping can all matter before the hammer falls.

The Neo Art House organises those questions into buyer-facing research so collectors can see what needs to be checked—and when a specialist should be brought in.
